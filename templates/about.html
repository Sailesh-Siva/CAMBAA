About Bone Age Prediction

This system predicts skeletal bone age using deep learning and medical image analysis.

The model is based on a MaxViT + CBAM architecture and incorporates gender information for improved prediction accuracy.

EigenCAM visualizations are generated to provide explainability by highlighting regions important for prediction.
