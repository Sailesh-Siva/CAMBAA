About CAMBAA Framework

The CAMBAA framework performs automated bone age assessment using YOLOv26-based hand localization, a MaxViT backbone with Residual CBAM attention, multimodal gender-aware feature fusion, and Support Vector Regression (SVR) for final prediction. Eigen-CAM explainability is additionally integrated to highlight clinically relevant skeletal regions influencing the prediction process.
